Privacy Notice

Effective date: August 8, 2026 · Consent Privacy Notice for Login with Amazon / Amazon Data Portability

R2Finance is a private household finance application operated for personal use by the household that controls this site (https://finance.i-liquid.be). It is not a public consumer product that sells or brokers customer data.

1. Who we are

R2Finance (“we”, “us”) provides a multi-account ledger and categorization tools for our household. Contact for privacy questions: jerome.ans@gmail.com.

2. What this notice covers

This notice explains how we handle information when you use R2Finance, and when you authorize Amazon to share data with R2Finance through Login with Amazon and Amazon Data Portability (for example physical order history).

3. Information we process

  • Account credentials for R2Finance — email and password (and multi-factor authentication where enabled) for household members we allow to sign in.
  • Financial ledger data — accounts, transactions, categories, payees, and related sync metadata stored in our AWS-hosted backend (for example DynamoDB).
  • Bank connection metadata — when you link a bank via Plaid, we store connection status and tokens needed for access; we do not sell bank credentials.
  • Amazon data you authorize — if you consent via Login with Amazon / Data Portability, we may receive order-related fields such as product names, ASINs, quantities, order identifiers, order dates, and amounts, solely to match and enrich your existing Amazon card/marketplace charges in the R2Finance ledger.

4. How we use Amazon-authorized data

  • Match bank or card transactions (for example descriptors like AMAZON MKTPL*) to Amazon order line items.
  • Show product titles and related order details inside R2Finance for household budgeting and categorization.
  • We do not sell this data.
  • We do not use it for advertising or third-party marketing.
  • We do not share it with unrelated third parties except as needed to run our infrastructure (AWS) or as required by law.

5. Storage and security

Data is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS), primarily in the us-east-1 region. Secrets (API keys, OAuth tokens, connection credentials) are stored in AWS Systems Manager Parameter Store and/or AWS Secrets Manager as SecureString values, not in public source control. Access to the live application is restricted to allowed household accounts.

6. Retention

Ledger and enrichment data are retained while we operate R2Finance for household bookkeeping. Amazon authorization tokens can be revoked by you in your Amazon account settings; we will stop using new Amazon data after revocation and may delete cached Amazon-derived fields on request.

7. Your choices

  • You may decline Login with Amazon / Data Portability consent; R2Finance will continue to work without Amazon order enrichment.
  • You may revoke Amazon access at any time through your Amazon account’s third-party application settings.
  • Household users may request deletion of R2Finance account data by contacting us at the email above.

8. Children

R2Finance is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this notice as the product evolves. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of R2Finance after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised notice for this household application.
